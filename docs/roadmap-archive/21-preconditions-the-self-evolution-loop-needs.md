## Preconditions the self-evolution loop needs before it is built

542. (issue #1221) **✅ LANDED (2026-09-20, merged `3fbb03ccb`; PR-time selection `227baab2f`, generation zero redefined `ad6a5025c`): `tests/fixtures/corpus_provenance.json` names a generation for every scoring document and oracle input, the census reports the loop-authored fraction per bucket, and `tools/corpus_provenance.py --check` in CI fails on an undeclared document, a stale line, or a corpus below its floor. Generation zero means pre-loop, not hand-written (issue #1397). THE ORACLE'S INDEPENDENCE DECAYS EVERY GENERATION, AND NOTHING MEASURES IT (2026-09-20, architecture review of the 512-541 wave; issue #1221).** The reason revl is an unusually good substrate for a repository-grounded loop is the reason item 146 exists: `src/revl/*.py` and `selfhost/*.rvl` are two implementations that must agree byte-for-byte, and the agreement is worth something **because the two were written independently**. Items 535 (issue #1205), 536 (issue #1206) and 537 (issue #1207) each address a different way a candidate can cheat the corpus: 535 derives tasks from repository artifacts rather than inventing them, 536 computes the reward from artifacts rather than from the candidate's prose, 537 withholds a scoring set the candidate cannot read. None of the three addresses the compounding one. Every generation writes model-authored code INTO the tree, and that tree is the next generation's training corpus, its differential oracle, and its scoring set at the same time. Independence is not a property the loop preserves; it is a stock the loop spends. Two mechanisms already in the tree make the spend invisible: `tests/fixtures/emit_py_corpus/` is GLOBBED, so a document added anywhere in it enters every byte-agreement projection automatically and silently, and `CORPUS_DIRS` in `tools/gate_reference_census.py` is walked with `rglob` over eight directories (this sentence said seven when it was written; `dogfood` joined `CORPUS_DIRS` on 2026-09-03 and the count has been eight ever since, which is what `tools/corpus_provenance.py` states), which is how the census reaches 846 programs without anyone maintaining a list. Both properties are good for coverage and both mean a generated document is indistinguishable from a hand-written one at the point where it is used as evidence. The failure is not that a model writes a corpus document; it is that the census cannot report what fraction of the evidence behind "the gate agrees with the reference" is evidence the engine authored about itself. Exit: every corpus document and every oracle input carries a provenance field naming the generation that authored it (a document that predates the loop is generation zero, which is not a claim that a person wrote it; CORRECTED 2026-09-29 per issue #1397), `tools/gate_reference_census.py` reports per-bucket what fraction of its programs are model-authored at or after a given generation, and a gate fails when any scoring corpus crosses a declared independence floor. The floor's value is a decision for the operator; the measurement is not optional, because a loop that cannot see this number cannot be argued with about it.

543. (issue #1222) **✅ LANDED (2026-09-20, merged `ddf0957a6`; pool and shadow entry `b19c7a590`): `src/revl/promotion_barrier.py` states the authority-before-measurement rule once, registers the promotion paths (controller, shadow promotion, peer pool, `revl canary`) and sweeps for an unregistered one; a widened reach with a clean comparison is refused by name and the unchanged control promotes on the same evidence, pinned by `tests/test_promotion_barrier_543.py`. THE AUTHORITY DIFF IS A PRECONDITION, NOT A STAGE, AND NO AMOUNT OF SLO EVIDENCE MAY BUY IT (2026-09-20, architecture review of the 512-541 wave; issue #1222).** The proposed evolution lifecycle runs observe, diagnose, propose, compile, admission-check, shadow-test, canary, measure, promote or roll back, and it lists the capability and reachability diff as one stage among those nine. That ordering is wrong in a way that only shows up on the changes that matter. A canary on one percent of traffic measures latency, refusal rate and answer quality, and it measures them on the paths that traffic happens to take; capability reach and taint edges are STATIC properties of the program, and the rare path that widens them is exactly the path a one-percent sample does not exhibit. Item 536's promotion bar already names the right conditions negatively, "no widened capability reach as measured by the G8 boundary", "no weakened refusal", "no unbounded resource path". What is missing is that they are stated as members of a list beside the SLO components rather than as a barrier in front of them. A candidate whose canary numbers are excellent and whose reachability diff is marginally non-empty must not be promotable, and as the lifecycle is drawn there is nothing that says so. This is the same shape as the finding the census exists to catch, one level up: a host reading a green as permission to run code the gate never admitted, where here the green is an SLO dashboard. Exit: the authority diff gates ENTRY to shadow and canary rather than being weighed beside their output, promotion refuses by name on a non-empty capability, taint or budget diff regardless of every measured objective, and the refusal is proven by a test in which a candidate with perfect canary numbers and one widened reach is refused, with a control candidate whose reach is unchanged promoted on the same evidence.

544. (issue #1223) **✅ LANDED (2026-09-20, merged `3f7dc04d8`): `src/revl/kernel_boundary.py` enumerates the admission kernel once as capability tokens, each citing its guarantee; `lower._check_kernel_boundary` refuses a component whose effective set meets it, naming both sets, and admits a disjoint control; retention is a kernel token, and an untrusted author may not declare a retention policy. Pinned by `tests/test_kernel_boundary_544.py`. Outside the exit: a bare `emission` with no declared reach is still read as disjoint, since the arm PR 1374 added was reverted (`edfa68cbe`). "THE SYSTEM MAY NOT EVOLVE THE ADMISSION KERNEL" IS A CAPABILITY, NOT A SENTENCE (2026-09-20, architecture review of the 512-541 wave; issue #1223).** Item 520 (issue #1194) carries the invariant the whole programme rests on: the system may evolve its behaviour but not unilaterally evolve the rules that govern its authority. Stated as a policy, that is a rule some later generation can propose a change to, and the loop's proposal channel is precisely the mechanism for proposing changes to rules. revl already owns the enforcement mechanism and it is not a policy: the attenuation product in `docs/capability-attenuation.md` accounts for services, realms, taints and budgets, and item 519 (issue #1193) is adding the model role to it. A generated component must be structurally unable to HOLD the capability that reaches the admission kernel, its gates, its recorded baselines and its formal model, in the same way and by the same product that stops any other component reaching past its declared authority. The kernel set has to be enumerated in one place rather than understood: `src/revl/{admission,attest,taint}.py`, `crates/revl-gate/**`, the census baseline and its `NEVER_BASELINED` list, `formal/**`, and the gate wiring in the required `lint` job. One concrete case belongs here rather than on the adaptive side of the ledger, and the wave currently files it on the wrong side: retention policy. A loop permitted to "update indexes and retention policies" as ordinary behaviour tuning is a loop that can extend its own `Retained[T, P]` deadline, which is an authority change wearing the clothes of a cache setting, and G-RETAIN is the guarantee it quietly relaxes. Item 532 measured how subtle that surface already is without any loop touching it: the retention refusal was argument-blind across a service seam, so a value past its deadline reached a provider's store with the compiler silent. Exit: the kernel capability set is enumerated in one file, a generated component declaring any intersection with it draws a NAMED refusal citing the guarantee it defends, retention deadline changes are on the kernel side of that boundary, and the refusal is proven non-vacuously with a control that admits a component whose declared set is disjoint.

545. (issue #1224) **✅ LANDED (2026-09-27, merged `1d17b3efd`; progress term `6ca746724`): `progress` is a conjunct in `tools/evolution_reward.py` over three monotone counters, and retention needs every counter read, none regressed and one strictly improved; a generation promotes only when a retained candidate advanced. The price: a real improvement no counter measures is not retained. THE REWARD IS ENTIRELY CONSERVATION, SO A CANDIDATE THAT CHANGES NOTHING SCORES PERFECTLY (2026-09-20, architecture review of the 512-541 wave; issue #1224).** Item 536 (issue #1206) enumerates eight reward components and every one of them is a preservation check: compiles, tests pass, no new false admits, no cross-tier divergence, byte stability of unrelated goldens, formal status, scope discipline, documentation accuracy. Each is correct and each is necessary. Together they define a reward whose maximum is attained by the empty diff, because nothing in the set rises when the system gets better and everything in it falls when the system breaks. A loop trained against that signal learns caution, not capability, and the cheapest policy that satisfies it is to propose nothing. The repository already maintains the counters the missing half needs, and they are monotone, mechanically produced and adversarial in the right direction. Measured on 2026-09-20 in one session: the census fail-open surface went from 21 documents to 9 and `false-reject` from 5 to 0 over 846 programs (`tools/gate_reference_census.py`, items 391 and 146, issues #106 and #84); item 146's fully-native chain went from 218 of 259 documents to 234 of 259 with every residual located in `selfhost/lower.rvl` rather than in an emitter, recomputed per tier rather than asserted; and the construct-reach report went from a check that could not fail to a shrink-only ledger holding 245 named gaps (item 533, issue #1203). Every one of those numbers can only move in one direction without a diff somebody reads, which is the same property item 536 correctly identifies as what makes the census usable as a reward at all. None of them was a reward component when this was filed; PR 1448 made them one, as `progress`. Exit: at least one progress component whose value is a monotone repository counter rather than a model's account of its own work, retention requires that component to be non-regressing, and promotion of a generation requires at least one accepted candidate to have improved it, so that a generation of empty diffs is recorded as a generation that did not advance rather than as a generation that scored well.

547. (issue #1233) **✅ LANDED (2026-09-26, merged `4650d7e2b`): the `path` rule judges every bare backticked path, declines three shapes by a rule legible in the citation, allowlists five of the six tools with reasons and corrects the sixth in item 536, and `tests/test_roadmap_claims_gate.py` reds on a rename, passes an unmodified tree and passes a control on both. The counts below describe the gate as filed. THE CITATION GATE JUDGES 94 OF 682 PATH CITATIONS, AND THE ROADMAP NAMES SIX TOOLS THAT DO NOT EXIST (2026-09-20, found while implementing item 536; issue #1233).** `tools/check_roadmap_claims.py` is the gate that keeps this document's citations honest, and it reports 261 claims over 4 rules with 0 stale. Its `path` rule collects only **`path:line`** citations, which its own docstring states as a deliberate decision about line drift: "a drifted line is not a false claim about the tree, it is a stale coordinate". That reasoning is sound and the consequence was not intended. A backticked path that never carried a line number is never turned into a claim, so it is never resolved against the tree. Measured on `f0557ce1`: this file holds **588** backticked path-shaped tokens with no `:line` and **94** with one, so the gate examines about 14 percent of its own citations. Of the 588 it does not examine, **23 resolve to nothing in the tree**, and six of those are claimed as revl tooling: canonical_demo.py, dsh_plugin_demo.py, gate_verdict_parity.py, mcp_client.py, timer_demo.py and web_server.py, all under `tools/`, none of which has ever existed here. The remaining seventeen are mostly illustrative or harness-side spellings (`./types.ts`, `root/app.rvl`, `src/components/*.rvl`, `mtier/*.rvl`), which is exactly why the fix is a judgement rule rather than a blanket assertion; `tools/roadmap_claim_allowlist.json` already exists for the deliberate cases. **How it was found is the argument for fixing it:** item 536 named `tools/gate_verdict_parity.py`, a file that never existed, as the machinery behind one of its eight reward components, the lane implementing that item went looking for the tool, and the reward now fails that component by name and asserts the file's absence so the assertion reds the day the tool arrives. Had the component been awarded instead, item 536's reward would have carried a component that could never fail, inside an item whose whole subject is that a reward must be a repository fact. That makes this the tenth check measured in this repository that runs on every pull request and cannot fail for the class of defect it was built for, alongside the construct-reach report (item 533), `docs/vision.md` (item 534), `gate_census`'s reach (issue #1215), the frontend pair that printed `skipping` forever, the SLO `measure()` that read `unmeasurable` for every objective, and the signed receipt whose `keyId` entered the body after the MAC. It is the one that stings, because it is the gate whose job is to keep the other claims honest. Exit: a bare backticked repository-path citation is judged for existence with line drift still out of scope, a deliberately foreign, illustrative or historical citation is distinguishable by a rule rather than by not being looked at, the six `tools/*.py` above are each restored, corrected or allowlisted with a stated reason, and the gate is proven to fire by a rename that reds it, an unmodified tree that passes, and a control that passes on both.
